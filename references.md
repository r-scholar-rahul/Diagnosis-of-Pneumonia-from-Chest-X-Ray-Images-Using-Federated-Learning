## 1. Federated Learning for Medical Image Analysis

### 1. Guan et al. — Federated Learning for Medical Image Analysis: A Survey

**Authors:** Hao Guan, Pew-Thian Yap, Andrea Bozoki, Mingxia Liu
**Year:** 2024
**Journal:** Pattern Recognition
**Volume:** 151
**Article:** 110424
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.patcog.2024.110424

**Relevance:**
A major survey of federated learning methods for medical image analysis. It discusses client-side methods, server-side methods, communication techniques, datasets, software platforms, challenges, and research opportunities.

---

### 2. Rehman et al. — Federated Learning for Medical Imaging Radiology

**Year:** 2023
**Publication:** British Institute of Radiology

**Relevance:**
Reviews the difference between state-of-the-art and state-of-the-practice federated learning for medical imaging. Important topics include privacy, security, data heterogeneity, aggregation, and clinical deployment.

---

### 3. Medical Imaging Applications of Federated Learning

**Year:** 2023
**Publication:** Open-access medical-imaging review

**Relevance:**
Reviews applications of federated learning across different medical imaging diseases and modalities and discusses privacy-preserving techniques such as differential privacy and homomorphic encryption.

---

### 4. Federated Learning in Radiomics: A Comprehensive Meta-Survey on Medical Image Analysis

**Year:** 2025
**Journal:** Computer Methods and Programs in Biomedicine
**Volume:** 267
**Article:** 108768
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cmpb.2025.108768

**Relevance:**
Provides a meta-survey of federated learning in medical imaging and identifies challenges including non-IID data, privacy, communication overhead, and clinical validation.

---

### 5. Advancements and Challenges of Federated Learning in Medical Imaging: A Systematic Literature Review

**Year:** 2026
**Journal:** Artificial Intelligence Review
**Article:** 87
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s10462-025-11489-z

**Relevance:**
Provides recent coverage of federated learning in medical imaging and discusses privacy frameworks, heterogeneity, performance bottlenecks, and the lack of clinical deployment benchmarks.

---

# 2. Pneumonia Detection Using Deep Learning

### 6. Siddiqi & Javaid — Deep Learning for Pneumonia Detection in Chest X-ray Images: A Comprehensive Survey

**Authors:** Raheel Siddiqi, Sameena Javaid
**Year:** 2024
**Journal:** Journal of Imaging
**Volume:** 10
**Issue:** 8
**Article:** 176
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.3390/jimaging10080176

**Relevance:**
A comprehensive survey of deep-learning approaches for pneumonia detection using chest X-ray images. The review covers CNNs, transfer learning, ensemble methods, GANs, explainable AI, and vision transformers.

# 5. Primary Research Paper

### 15. Diagnosis of Pneumonia from Chest X-Ray Images Using Federated Learning

**Authors:** Asadi Srinivasulu, Saurabh Kumar, Rohit Chowdhury, Rahul Mahto, Anupam Agrawal
**Year:** 2025
**Book:** Data Science and Applications
**Pages:** 31–46
**Publisher:** Springer

**DOI:**
https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-96-1188-1_3

**Relevance:**
This is the primary research paper for this repository. It investigates pneumonia diagnosis from chest X-ray images using federated learning and evaluates VGG-16, ResNet-50, and InceptionV3.

**Reported accuracies:**

* VGG-16 — 90%
* ResNet-50 — 87%
* InceptionV3 — 89%

**Paper:**
https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-981-96-1188-1_3

5. Confirm DOI.
6. Open the DOI or publisher page.
7. Confirm the link points to the same paper.
8. Record the verification source.
